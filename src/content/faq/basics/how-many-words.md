title: How many words are there in Toki Pona?
faq_group: 1
faq_index: 3
published: true
image: words-how-many.png
simple: Depending on the speaker, 120-140!
---

The figure you’ll hear most often is 120 words, but most speakers use somewhere **around 130 words**. You may also find speakers who use closer to 120 words, or speakers who use closer to 140 words. 

Each speaker develops their own preferences for words they like to use, and most will recognize more words than they use themselves, which makes pinning down a specific number tricky. 

It's uncommon, but a small amount of speakers do fall outside this range, using less than 120 words, or using more than 140 words. At that point, that speaker is using more words than many other speakers will understand.
