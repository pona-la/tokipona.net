title: How do I learn Toki Pona?
simple:
  "Learn the grammar and vocab with a course and then practice, practice, practice, practice!'"
---


**Everyone has different learning styles and preferences.** Do you follow one course, or do you jump between a bunch of them at the same time? Do you drill the vocabulary with flashcards, or do you pick up the words as they come? Do you prioritize learning through immersion, or learning from lectures? Do you also learn Sitelen Pona, the original script that represents each word with a symbol? Different learning materials will suit different styles, and we've compiled some recommended options over at [Resources](/resources/#courses). If you've never tried to learn a language before, learning Toki Pona is one way you can experiment with which styles work for you. 

You can memorize all of Toki Pona's words very quickly, but once you have, you're still at the start of your learning journey. **Learning to structure sentences correctly, to use the words creatively, and to and to understand others when they speak Toki Pona, all of these things take practice.** Many speakers practice through journalling, reading Toki Pona stories, listening to Toki Pona music and podcasts, translating their favourite songs or short stories, etc. Probably the most effective form of practice is reaching out to other Toki Pona speakers, since this is where you will get feedback. 

Check out our [Resources](/resources/#communities) page for links to some communities. Toki Pona speakers are generally very eager to help new learners out, as we were all once newbies ourselves! You're welcome to join these communities and participate, or observe as others ask questions and practice, whatever you're comfortable with.
