Toki Pona has been historically marketed as a tool for simplifying your thoughts and improving your mental health, but this claim is more than marketing than science. **There are no studies exploring this question. There is no evidence either way.**
 
Although not specific to Toki Pona, [hobbies are known to provide some stress relief, social connection and improve one's quality of life](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40489730/). It is possible for Toki Pona to improve your mental health, as it is possible for any hobby to do so. 

We've linked to a page where you may read about [some experiences that people have had with Toki Pona](/perspectives/), keeping in mind the sample of people that saw our request for submissions were more likely to respond positively, and keeping in mind that these are anecdotes. 

While there are positive anecdotes, there are also negative anecdotes out there:  learning a new language can be joyful and stressful, meeting new people can be fun and dreadful, and you can find both new friends and new drama in a new community.  

Each story is different, each mind is different, and we can't predict what the outcome will be.
