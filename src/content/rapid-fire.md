
### Does Toki Pona have verb conjugation and tenses?

No, Toki Pona has no verb conjugation and does not bake time information into its verbs. 


### What does "Toki Pona" mean?

In  _toki_ means 'language, communication, speech,' and  _pona_ means 'good, positive quality.' Combined, it is like 'good language' or 'pleasant communication.' When speaking Toki Pona, the phrase  _toki pona_ sometimes refers to the language itself and sometimes  refers to its meaning. For example, if you just delivered a speech,  "_sina toki pona!_", or "You communicated well!"


### How to say, "I love you" in Toki Pona?

"mi olin e sina." 

### Are there native speakers of Toki Pona?

There are no confirmed cases yet. Previously speculated cases have so far been hoaxes. 

### What syllables are forbidden in Toki Pona?

The following combinations are forbidden: *wu, wo, ji, ti, nm, nn.*

### Does Toki Pona have swear words?

There are no dedicated swear words, but emotionally exclaiming the words *pakala* (destruction), *ike* (negative, evil), *jaki* (unhygienic) along with modifiers to intensify the degree of negativity, usually gets the job done. *ike sewi a!*

### When is Toki Pona's birthday?

August 8th, 2001. The community celebrates with a 24-hour livestream called *suno pi toki pona,* or [Toki Pona Day](https://suno.pona.la/). 

### Does Toki Pona have a sign language? 

[Luka Pona Sign Language](https://sona.pona.la/wiki/luka_pona) (LPSL) is used within the Toki Pona community. It shares Toki Pona's vocabulary, but has a totally different grammar system, borrowing spatial grammar concepts from natural sign languages that better suit the visual medium. 



### Does Toki Pona have an ISO code? 

Toki Pona's ISO 639-3 code is <code>tok</code>


### Is Toki Pona on Duolingo?

No, and there are no plans to add Toki Pona to Duolingo. Alternative Duolingo-like community-made resources include the [Wasona course](https://wasona.com/), and the [Unofficial Duolingo Stories](https://duostories.org/tok-en) project.

### How do colours work in Toki Pona?

*loje*, *jelo*, *laso*, *walo* and *pimeja* (reddish, yellowish, tealish, lightish and darkish) are combined together to make more colours, when necessary.  





### What does *kijetesantakalu* mean in Toki Pona?

Toki Pona's words are mostly short and cover a broad range of meanings. *kijetesantakalu* is a hyper-specific joke word meaning 'raccoon' that was created on April Fool's day in 2009. It remains a popular meme/mascot in the community. 

### Does ChatGPT (and other "AI" models) know Toki Pona? 

Not very well. There's not enough good training data for them. They are prone to hallucinations when teaching Toki Pona grammar in English, and produce errors when speaking Toki Pona. They have some success deciphering basic phrases, but anything more than that, hallucinations and inaccuracies are frequent. 


### Why is Toki Pona often in lowercase?

Toki Pona is written in lowercase, including words at the start of a sentence. Capitalization is only used to indicate loaned proper names, like *ma Kanata* (land + Canada) or *toki Epanja* (language + Espanol), or *jan Elen* (person + Helen).
